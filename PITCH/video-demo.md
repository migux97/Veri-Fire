# Demo video

Walkthrough of VeriFire on Solana devnet (2:57, English voice-over and subtitles): a company buys a batch and pays it
with Solana Pay, the batch with its public and secret QR codes, the record on Solana Explorer, a buyer activating a
warranty with the sealed QR, the owner history and its on-chain certificate, and the transfer link.

**Watch it here:** [VeriFire demo EN.mp4](VeriFire%20demo%20EN.mp4) (subtitles: [VeriFire demo EN.srt](VeriFire%20demo%20EN.srt))

The pitch (1:58) is [VeriFire pitch EN.mp4](VeriFire%20pitch%20EN.mp4), following [pitch-script.md](pitch-script.md).
