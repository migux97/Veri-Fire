# Pitch script (2:00)

About 300 words: it fits in two minutes at a brisk pace. If a take runs long, cut the "Why Solana" block to its first sentence. Each block says what is on screen.

**0:00 to 0:20 · The problem** (slide: +$500B)

A buyer abroad holding a bottle of premium Argentine Malbec has no way to know if it is real, and the winery has no way
to prove where that bottle came from. Counterfeit goods move close to half a trillion dollars a year, according to the
OECD and the EU's intellectual property office.

**0:20 to 0:35 · The insight** (slide: one token, two QRs)

A QR code alone can be copied. What cannot be copied is a secret sealed under the capsule that only appears when the
bottle is opened. So every unit gets two QRs: a public one to verify, and a sealed one to claim it.

**0:35 to 1:00 · The product** (devnet screenshots)

The winery registers a lot and prints the labels. In the store, anyone scans the public QR and sees the lot and the
verified winery, with no app and no account. After buying, the customer opens the seal, scans the secret QR, signs in
with an email, and the bottle and its warranty are theirs on Solana. If they resell it, ownership moves with it.

**1:00 to 1:15 · Why Solana**

Each unit is an account in our Anchor program on devnet. The seal is checked on-chain with Solana's native ed25519
verification, so it can only be claimed once. We pay the fees, so buyers never need SOL, and wineries pay per lot in
USDC with Solana Pay.

**1:15 to 1:35 · Market and pricing** (slides: business model, go-to-market)

We start with small and mid-size exporting wineries in Cafayate, Salta, who need traceability they can show their
importers. We charge per labeled bottle within a lot, paid in USDC; the price is a hypothesis we are validating with
wineries.

**1:35 to 1:50 · Team**

Franco and Emanuel build the program and the app. Jorge runs marketing and the relationship with wineries. We are in
Salta, a few hours from Cafayate.

**1:50 to 2:00 · Next milestone**

Our next step is to label a real lot with a pilot winery and measure how many buyers verify and claim their bottle.
Thank you.
