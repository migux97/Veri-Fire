# Pitch script (2:00)

About 310 words: it fits in two minutes at a brisk pace. If a take runs long, cut the "Why Solana" block to its first
sentence.

**0:00 to 0:20 · The problem** (slide: +$500B)

When you buy a pair of limited sneakers, a graphics card or a watch, you have to take the seller's word that it is
real, and once it is resold, nobody can prove where it came from. Counterfeit goods move close to half a trillion
dollars a year, according to the OECD and the EU's intellectual property office.

**0:20 to 0:35 · The insight** (slide: one token, two QRs)

A serial number or a QR printed on a box can be copied. What cannot be copied is a secret sealed inside the box that
only appears when it is opened. So every unit gets two QRs: a public one to verify, and a sealed one to claim it.

**0:35 to 1:00 · The product** (devnet screenshots)

The brand registers a batch and prints the labels. In the store, anyone scans the public QR and sees the product, the
batch and the verified brand, with no app and no account. After buying, the customer opens the box, scans the secret
QR, signs in with an email, and the product and its warranty are theirs on Solana. If they resell it, ownership moves
with it.

**1:00 to 1:15 · Why Solana**

Each unit is an account in our Anchor program on devnet. The seal is checked on-chain with Solana's native ed25519
verification, so it can only be claimed once. We pay the fees, so buyers never need SOL, and brands pay per batch in
USDC with Solana Pay.

**1:15 to 1:35 · Market and go-to-market** (slides: business model, go-to-market)

We focus on high-value products that are not mass-produced: sneakers, electronics, watches, perfume, wine. We start
with wineries in Cafayate, Salta, where lot traceability for export gives us a concrete entry point, and then expand to
other brands and distributors. We charge per registered product, paid in USDC.

**1:35 to 1:50 · Team**

Franco and Emanuel build the program and the app. Jorge runs marketing and the relationship with brands.

**1:50 to 2:00 · Next milestone**

Our next step is a pilot with a first brand labeling real products, measuring how many buyers verify and claim them.
Thank you.
