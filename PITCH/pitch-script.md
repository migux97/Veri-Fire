# Pitch script (1:58)

What is said in the [pitch video](https://youtu.be/xkL3zD88C2E), over the slides of
[VeriFire Pitch Deck (EN).pptx](VeriFire%20Pitch%20Deck%20(EN).pptx). Times are those of the video.

| Time | Slide | What is said |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Problem: +$500B | When you buy a pair of limited sneakers, a graphics card or a watch, you have to take the seller's word that it is real, and once it is resold, nobody can prove where it came from. Counterfeit goods move over five hundred billion dollars a year. |
| 0:19 | Product tokenization | A serial number or a QR printed on a box can be copied. What cannot be copied is a secret sealed inside the box that only appears when it is opened. |
| 0:31 | One token, two QR | So every unit gets two QRs: a public one to verify, and a sealed one to claim it. |
| 0:38 | Claim your product | After buying, the customer opens the box and scans the secret QR, signs in with an email, and the product and its warranty are theirs on Solana. If they resell it, ownership moves with it. |
| 0:54 | Product tokenization (Solana) | Each unit is an account in our Anchor program on devnet. The seal is checked on-chain with Solana's native ed25519 verification, so it can only be claimed once. We pay the fees, so buyers never need SOL. |
| 1:13 | Go-to-market | We focus on high-value products that are not mass-produced: sneakers, electronics, watches, perfume, wine. We start with wineries in Cafayate, Salta, where lot traceability for export gives us a concrete entry point. |
| 1:29 | Business model | We charge per registered product, paid in USDC. |
| 1:33 | Team | I built the Solana program and the backend, Jorge the company panel, and Emanuel the landing. Jorge and I come from Argentina Builders, and we're based in Salta, a few hours from the Cafayate wineries we start with. |
| 1:47 | Go-to-market | Our next step is a pilot with a first brand labeling real products, measuring how many buyers verify and claim them. |
| 1:56 | Logo | Thank you. |

Pirated Goods*), cited on the slide.
